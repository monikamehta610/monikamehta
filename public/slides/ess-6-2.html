6.2.1 & 6.2.2

Climate System & Anthropogenic Acceleration

Key UnderstandingClimate represents 30+ year average weather patterns. Anthropogenic CO₂ emissions have accelerated rapidly since the post-1950 Great Acceleration.

🌤️ Weather vs. Climate

Weather = day-to-day atmospheric conditions. Climate = 30-year statistical average of temperature, precipitation, and wind.

📈 Post-1950 Acceleration

Pre-industrial CO₂ levels remained at ~280 ppm for millennia. Post-WWII industrial expansion drove concentrations to 425+ ppm today.

6.2.4 & 6.2.5

Enhanced Greenhouse Effect & Ecosystem Shifts

Key UnderstandingExcess GHGs enhance radiative heat trapping, resulting in global warming, ocean thermal expansion, coral bleaching, and poleward biome shifts.

🪸 Marine Ecosystem Collapse

Warming sea surface temperatures cause corals to expel zooxanthellae (bleaching). Combined with ocean acidification ($\text{CO}_2 \rightarrow$ carbonic acid), reef structures dissolve.

🌲 Poleward Biome Shifts

Species ranges shift toward higher latitudes and altitudes. Mountain species face "summit traps" with nowhere higher to migrate.

6.2.7

Climate Feedback Loops

Key UnderstandingClimate systems feature positive feedbacks (amplifying warming) and negative feedbacks (dampening warming). Positive feedbacks dominate today.

🔥 Positive Feedback (Self-Reinforcing)

Ice-Albedo: Warming $\rightarrow$ Ice melts $\rightarrow$ Dark ocean absorbs heat $\rightarrow$ More melting.
Permafrost: Warming $\rightarrow$ Thaw $\rightarrow$ Methane released $\rightarrow$ More warming.

☁️ Negative Feedback (Stabilizing)

Cloud Albedo: Warming $\rightarrow$ Evaporation $\rightarrow$ High low-level cloud cover $\rightarrow$ Reflects solar radiation $\rightarrow$ Cooling effect.

Climate Change Planetary BoundaryHL

Key UnderstandingThe safe operating limit for climate change is 350 ppm CO₂ and +1.0 W/m² radiative forcing. Both boundaries have been crossed.
Indicator ParameterPlanetary Safe BoundaryCurrent Observed StatusRisk Threshold
Atmospheric CO₂350 ppm425+ ppmEXCEEDED (High Risk Zone)
Radiative Forcing+1.0 W/m²+2.3 W/m²EXCEEDED (High Risk Zone)
Global Temp Anomaly+1.5°C above pre-industrial+1.1°C to +1.2°CApproaching Tipping Points

Paris Agreement Targets (2015)

Legally binding international treaty committing nations to limit global warming to well below 2.0°C, pursuing efforts to limit increase to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels to prevent catastrophic climate tipping points.

Exam Preparation

Key Takeaways & Exam Tips

Must-Know Concepts

  • Proxy Data Evidence: Vostok ice cores prove CO₂ and global temperature move in lockstep; current 425+ ppm is unprecedented in 800,000 years.
  • Positive vs Negative Feedback: Ice-albedo and permafrost methane release are positive (amplifying); cloud reflection is negative (cooling).
  • HL Planetary Boundary: CO₂ boundary (350 ppm) has been exceeded; Paris Agreement targets +1.5°C limit.
